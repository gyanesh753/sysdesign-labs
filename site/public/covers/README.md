# Covers

Put a cover image here to show it on the shelf instead of the drawn cover.

- Name it after the book's `id` in `course.yaml`: `ddia.jpg`, `aie.png`, and so on.
- `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` and `.webp` are picked up.
- A 2:3 portrait image looks best.

Image files in this folder are ignored by git, so they stay on your machine. A book with no image here
gets a cover drawn by the site in its accent colour.
